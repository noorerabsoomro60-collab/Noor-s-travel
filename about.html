About Noor Travel

Creating unforgettable travel experiences since 2010

Our Story

Founded in 2010, Noor Travel began as a small boutique travel agency with a simple mission: to create personalized, unforgettable travel experiences that go beyond the ordinary.

What started as a passion project between two travel enthusiasts has grown into a premier travel company serving thousands of clients worldwide. Our name "Noor" means "light" in Arabic, reflecting our commitment to illuminating the path to extraordinary journeys.

Today, we partner with the finest hotels, airlines, and local experts across 50+ countries to deliver seamless travel experiences that create lifelong memories.
